# Pale Cartographer

## Overview

A converted free merchant repurposed as a survey vessel, the Pale Cartographer catalogs pretech ruins and Mandate-era infrastructure across the sector. Its presence in Kestrel is no accident — the Surveyors are quietly investigating both the orbital elevator ruins on Kestrel II and the theft of one of their own patrol craft, now sailing under a different name.

## History

- Dispatched to Kestrel System to assess the civil war's threat to pretech sites in the region
- Has quietly reached out to Governor Osk's administration for access and protection

## Hooks & Secrets

- The Surveyors know the [[Nightshade Vow]] is their stolen patrol craft and are building a case to reclaim it
- Archivist Toll suspects the pretech cache on Kestrel III's moons predates the Mandate entirely and wants it studied before either faction weaponizes it
