# Wayward Cairn

## Overview

A free merchant running the Vesper Reach corridor on a steady loop, carrying cargo, mail, and the occasional passenger between Vesper Hollow and Port Kestrel. Merrin keeps it scrupulously legal, which has made the Cairn one of the few ships every faction in Vesper System trusts enough to leave alone.

## Crew

- **Captain** — [[Captain Joss Merrin]]

## Cargo & Notable Gear

- General cargo: mail, spare parts, medical supplies, and the occasional refugee moving quietly between systems
- A well-worn set of charts noting where Vesper Reach's instability tends to bite hardest

## History

- Purchased secondhand by the Free Traders Association specifically to open the Vesper corridor once the direct Kestrel routes grew too dangerous
- Has never missed a scheduled run, a point of quiet pride for its crew

## Hooks & Secrets

- Merrin has picked up more cross-faction gossip running this route than anyone in Vesper System realizes
- The Association wants an escort for a run past the edge of The Driftfield, where salvage traffic has started crowding the safe lanes
