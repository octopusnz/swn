# Widow's Toll

## Overview

A former frontier-navy patrol boat, stripped of its markings and repainted matte black, the Widow's Toll is Red Veil's enforcement arm in the Driftfield — fast enough to run down scavengers who won't take a hint, armored enough to shrug off the debris field's hazards.

## Crew

- **Captain** — [[Enforcer Dashiell Corr]]
- Eight crew, mostly ex-military muscle brought in to enforce Red Veil's claim over the Driftfield's best salvage

## Cargo & Notable Gear

- Salvaged hull plate and scavenged components awaiting transfer to the Anchorage's black market
- A locked hold nobody outside Corr's inner circle has been allowed to inspect

## History

- Acquired by the Cartel through channels Corr has never fully explained, even to Kael
- Has run off at least three independent scavenger crews from claims Corr wanted for Red Veil

## Hooks & Secrets

- The locked hold may contain salvage from the Vesper Array itself — pulled from the Driftfield's oldest wreckage and not yet reported to Kael
- Crews who've crossed the Widow's Toll in the Driftfield have a way of not being heard from again
