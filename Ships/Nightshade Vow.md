# Nightshade Vow

## Overview

A stripped-down corvette running dark paint and a spoofed transponder, the Nightshade Vow is the Red Veil Cartel's favored ship for running weapons and spike drive components into the Shattered Belt under cover of the mining traffic. Fast, armored, and willing to fight its way out of trouble.

## Crew

- **Captain** — [[Captain Vashti Kael]]
- Six-person crew, mostly ex-Mandate military washouts loyal to Kael personally

## Cargo & Notable Gear

- Falsified cargo manifests disguising weapon crates as mining equipment
- A cache of military-grade spike drive components bound for the Ashvale Coalition

## History

- Formerly a Mandate Remnant Surveyors patrol craft, stolen by Kael's crew during a refueling stop years ago
- Has slipped past Port Kestrel customs at least a dozen times using forged Free Traders Association credentials

## Hooks & Secrets

- The weapons Kael is running trace back to the pretech cache rumored on one of Kestrel III's moons
- Kael has a standing bounty from the Mandate Remnant Surveyors for the theft of this very ship
