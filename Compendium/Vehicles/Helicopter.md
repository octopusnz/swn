## Description

Used on TL3 worlds where a lack of airfields or treacherous local terrain prohibits the use of fixed-wing craft. Their ability to hover over an area or drop onto a tightly-constrained landing zone makes them particular favorites of quick-response military forces and groups in need of fast fire support. This model represents a medium-weight civilian craft that might be repurposed by militia or adventurers.
