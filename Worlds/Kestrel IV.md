# Kestrel IV

## Overview

A frozen, wind-scoured world at the edge of Kestrel's habitable zone. Once home to a small research outpost, now dark and unclaimed. Nobody currently in Kestrel System has bothered to find out why it was abandoned or what, if anything, was left behind.

## Geography & Environment

- Perpetual ice sheets and glacial plains, with violent katabatic windstorms off the polar caps
- A handful of exposed rock ridges near the equator are the only ground not permanently under ice

## Settlements

- **The Outpost** — a shuttered research station, structurally intact but powered down and unvisited in years

## Hooks & Secrets
