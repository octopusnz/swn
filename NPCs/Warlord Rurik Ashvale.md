# Warlord Rurik Ashvale

## Overview

A former Council militia commander who broke away with half his regiment when the Council cut Ashvale highlands funding to pay for Port Kestrel's defenses. Now he leads the Coalition from a fortress built into a Mandate-era bunker, and he has no intention of ever bending the knee to New Kestrel City again.

## Appearance & Personality

- Built like the soldier he still is; wears mismatched Council-surplus armor as a trophy
- Charismatic to his own people, ruthless to anyone he considers an occupier
- Genuinely believes he's fighting for Ashvale's survival, not just his own power

## Relationships & Factions

- Commands the Ashvale Coalition from Ashvale Hold
- At war with [[Governor Lian Osk]] and the Kestrel Corporate Council
- Buys weapons from the Red Veil Cartel, run by [[Captain Vashti Kael]], though he doesn't fully trust them

## Hooks & Secrets

- Doesn't know the weapons he's buying trace back to a pretech cache the Mandate Remnant Surveyors want recovered intact
- Would consider a truce if offered a genuinely fair settlement — but no one has offered one yet
- Ashvale Hold's bunker walls hide old Mandate systems even he hasn't fully explored
