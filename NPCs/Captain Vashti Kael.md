# Captain Vashti Kael

![[Images/vashtikael.jpg]]

## Overview

Captain of the Nightshade Vow and the Red Veil Cartel's point woman for the Kestrel run, Kael has made a fortune keeping both sides of the Kestrel II civil war armed and fighting. She's not loyal to either faction — only to the profit margin the war keeps generating.

## Appearance & Personality

- Former Mandate Remnant Surveyors navigator, still wears a modified Surveyor flight jacket with the insignia cut off
- Cold and businesslike; treats every negotiation like a transaction, including threats
- Keeps her crew fiercely loyal through generous cuts and brutal discipline

## Relationships & Factions

- Runs weapons for the Red Veil Cartel into the Kestrel II civil war
- Sells to [[Warlord Rurik Ashvale]] and the Ashvale Coalition, though she'd sell to the Council just as readily for the right price
- Stole the [[Nightshade Vow]] from the Mandate Remnant Surveyors, who now have a bounty on her

## Hooks & Secrets

- The Mandate Remnant Surveyors, via Archivist Renn Toll aboard the [[Pale Cartographer]], are quietly building a case to reclaim her ship
- Knows the true source of the weapons she's running: a pretech cache hidden on one of Kestrel III's moons
- Would defect from the Cartel for the right offer — she has no real loyalty to Red Veil either
