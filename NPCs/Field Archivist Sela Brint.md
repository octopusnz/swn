# Field Archivist Sela Brint

## Overview

Dispatched by Archivist Renn Toll to investigate why Vesper Reach has grown so unreliable, Brint found her answer within weeks: a dying Mandate relay array anchored to one of Vesper's Shroud's moons, its systems failing after centuries without maintenance. What she's found since has raised more questions than it answered.

## Appearance & Personality

- Methodical to the point of obsession, rarely without a data slate recording every reading
- More at ease around old Mandate hardware than around people
- Growing increasingly uneasy about how much of the array's original purpose remains unclear

## Relationships & Factions

- Reports to Archivist Renn Toll aboard the [[Pale Cartographer]] on behalf of the [[Mandate Remnant Surveyors]]
- Stationed near the Vesper Array on [[Vesper's Shroud]], passing through [[Vesper Hollow]] for supplies and courier contact
- Wary of [[Enforcer Dashiell Corr]]'s salvage crews working too close to what may be pieces of the array itself

## Hooks & Secrets

- Confirmed the array's decline is the true cause of Vesper Reach's instability — and that it's accelerating
- Suspects part of the installation predates the Mandate entirely, echoing the rumors around the vault on Kestrel III's moons
- Some of the array's automated systems are still drawing power from a source she hasn't been able to identify, and they've started responding to her survey drones
