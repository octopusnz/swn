# Enforcer Dashiell Corr

## Overview

"Dash" Corr runs Red Veil's Driftfield operation on Vashti Kael's authority — salvage on the surface, smuggling underneath. Ex-Mandate military like most of Kael's inner circle, Corr treats the wreck field like a personal fiefdom and has little patience for independent scavengers who don't take the hint to move on.

## Appearance & Personality

- Broad-shouldered and scarred, wears salvaged void armor plating like a badge of rank
- Blunt and businesslike, more comfortable giving orders than negotiating
- Fiercely loyal to Kael personally, less so to the Cartel as an institution

## Relationships & Factions

- Runs [[Red Veil Cartel]] salvage and smuggling operations out of [[The Driftfield]], captaining the [[Widow's Toll]]
- Reports to [[Captain Vashti Kael]] and fences goods through the Anchorage on [[Vesper Hollow]]
- Keeps a wary, unspoken truce with [[Warden Petra Voss]]'s Council listening post — neither wants a fight neither can afford

## Hooks & Secrets

- Has been steering the best salvage claims to Red Veil-friendly crews and freezing out independents who won't cooperate
- Doesn't know it, but some of the "old wreckage" his crews have been quietly picking apart may be pieces of the Vesper Array itself
- A scavenger crew that pushed back against his claims went dark three weeks ago — Corr knows exactly what happened and isn't talking
